# Company Responsible AI Guidelines

## Principles
- Fairness: test relevant groups and investigate unexplained disparities.
- Human accountability: a named employee remains accountable for high-impact decisions.
- Transparency: disclose meaningful AI use and explain limitations.
- Privacy: collect only necessary data; restrict access and retention; never upload confidential data to unapproved AI services.
- Accuracy: verify important outputs against trusted sources.
- Hallucination control: use approved knowledge sources, ask the model to state uncertainty and require human review for consequential claims.
- Safety: limit tool permissions and require approval before external or irreversible actions.
- Intellectual property: use approved data and respect licenses and copyright.
- Monitoring: track quality, bias, incidents, latency and cost.
- Incident response: report, investigate, document and correct harmful outcomes.

## Before deployment
- [ ] Purpose and risk level documented
- [ ] Data source, consent and retention reviewed
- [ ] Bias and accuracy tests completed
- [ ] Hallucination and safety tests completed
- [ ] Human review and appeal process defined
- [ ] Privacy and security review completed
- [ ] Monitoring owner and rollback plan assigned
- [ ] User disclosure and limitations prepared

## Prohibited practices
Do not use AI as the sole decision-maker for hiring, lending, health or other high-impact decisions. Do not enter confidential information into unapproved tools. Do not present unverified generated content as fact.
