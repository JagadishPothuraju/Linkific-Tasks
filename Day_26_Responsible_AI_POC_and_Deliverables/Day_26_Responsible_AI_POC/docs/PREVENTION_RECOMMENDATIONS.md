# Prevention Recommendations

1. Use representative, documented datasets and identify underrepresented groups.
2. Use validated, job-related features; avoid irrelevant proxies such as gender-coded club names.
3. Measure selection rates, error rates and performance across relevant groups where lawful and appropriate.
4. Conduct pre-deployment impact assessments and independent audits.
5. Keep qualified humans accountable for high-impact decisions; provide review and appeal paths.
6. Tell affected people when AI is used and explain its role and limitations.
7. Minimize personal data, restrict access, encrypt data and define retention periods.
8. Monitor drift, complaints and group-level outcomes after launch.
9. Maintain dataset documentation, model cards, test results and change logs.
10. Pause or roll back systems when harmful disparities are detected.
