Architecture explanation:
1. Streamlit is the presentation layer used by the user.
2. Upload validation checks the extension and file size.
3. The extraction layer reads PDF, DOCX or TXT content.
4. The JD input is validated separately.
5. Skill identification searches for configured skill terms in both texts.
6. TF-IDF converts the two texts into feature vectors.
7. Cosine similarity calculates text similarity.
8. The dashboard presents matched skills, missing skills and the similarity score.
9. A human reviewer interprets the evidence; the score must not make an automatic hiring decision.
