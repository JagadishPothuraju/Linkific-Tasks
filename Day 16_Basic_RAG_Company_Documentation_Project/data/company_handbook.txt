ACME COMPANY HANDBOOK

Working Hours
Employees normally work from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible schedules must be approved by the employee's manager.

Leave Policy
Submit planned leave requests at least five working days in advance. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.

IT Support
For laptop, account, or network problems, create a ticket through the internal IT help desk. Include a short description, device name, and error message. Never share passwords in support tickets.

Expense Reimbursement
Submit expense claims with receipts within 30 days of purchase. Manager approval is required before Finance processes reimbursement.

Information Security
Use multi-factor authentication where available. Never share passwords or access tokens. Report suspicious emails to the security team.
