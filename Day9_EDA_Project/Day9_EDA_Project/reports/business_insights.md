# Five Academic / Business Insights

Dataset: cleaned student dataset from Day 8 (n=8). These observations describe this small sample only.

1. **Overall performance:** Average marks are 82.25/100, with a median of 84.00.
2. **Department performance:** AI & ML has the highest average marks (83.33/100) in this sample.
3. **Attendance:** AI & ML has the highest average attendance (92.00%).
4. **Attendance and marks:** The Pearson correlation is 0.361. This indicates a positive association in this sample, not proof that attendance causes higher marks.
5. **Performance range:** Marks range from 67 to 95, a difference of 28 points. Students at the lower end may benefit from additional academic support, after checking the wider context.

**Limitation:** There are only eight records and the data is a practice dataset. Do not generalize these results to the whole institution.
