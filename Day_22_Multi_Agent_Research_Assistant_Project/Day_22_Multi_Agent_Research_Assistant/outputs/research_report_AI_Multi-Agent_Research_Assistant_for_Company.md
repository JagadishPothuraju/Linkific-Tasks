## Research Notes

A coordinator plans the workflow, assigns work, checks completion, and manages hand-offs.\n- A research agent gathers relevant information from approved sources and records source details. Local knowledge bases are useful for demonstrations but may be incomplete or outdated.\n- Shared state stores the topic, plan, research notes, analysis, critique, draft, status, and communication log. Agents read and update relevant fields.\n- Communication can follow a sequence: Coordinator to Research Agent, Research Agent to Analyzer, Analyzer to Critic and Writer, Critic to Writer, Writer to Coordinator.\n- An analyzer organizes information, identifies themes, compares evidence, and highlights gaps. Analysis should distinguish facts from assumptions.\n- A critic reviews clarity, evidence coverage, unsupported claims, contradictions, missing context, and safety concerns. Criticism should be constructive and actionable.
